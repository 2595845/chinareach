How it works

Three steps, about two minutes.

  1. Join the beta — pick a plan above and email us. No card needed for the free 7-day beta.
  2. Get your config — we send a standard WireGuard configuration and plain-English setup guides for Windows, macOS, Android and iOS.
  3. Connect — one tap. Only your China-bound traffic is routed through the tunnel; everything else stays on your normal connection.

FAQ

Is this a real mainland IP, or a virtual location? Real. Your connection exits from servers physically located in mainland China. Many large VPN brands list a "China" location that is actually routed through Hong Kong or Singapore — Chinese platforms detect and block those. Ours is the genuine article.

Why not just use the existing Chinese accelerator apps? They work — if you read Chinese, have a Chinese app-store account, and can pay with Alipay or WeChat as a new overseas user. ChinaReach is the same idea, built English-first: English app guidance, English support, and card/PayPal checkout.

Do you log my traffic? No. We keep only the minimum account data needed to run your subscription. See our Privacy Policy.

What may I not use it for? Anything illegal in your jurisdiction or in China, spam, fraud, or attacks on networks. See our Acceptable Use Policy.

Can I cancel? Anytime, in one email. Paid plans are refunded pro-rata within the first 14 days.
